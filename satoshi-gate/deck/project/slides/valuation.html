Break-even growth 31.3% p.a. for 10 years with zero churn and no drag. WACC 11–14% moves base value ₹2,127 → ₹1,265 Cr. Add the drag NPV (₹4,238 Cr) and 100% ownership is negative below 40% growth.

Is the valuation supportable?

  • Not as an unconditional ₹4,000 Cr
  • Only as a cap reached through retained ARR: ₹3,127 Cr total at 30% churn, ₹2,690 Cr at 45%
  • If Liminal delivers 30% growth, the cap sits 16% below the then-DCF of ~₹4,756 Cr

Churn tolerance (answer to 5.2)

  • At the headline price, any churn above 0% makes the deal unattractive: the price is unsupported before churn
  • Under our structure the price self-corrects down to 55% retention
  • Below 55% retention (churn > 45%) control is never exercised

Structuring response

  • Deferred consideration · retention earn-out · CVR-style payout on retained ARR · valuation cap at headline
  • Fundamental value of 26%: ₹456 Cr (base) – ₹951 Cr (bull); the ₹1,040 Cr ceiling carries a disclosed premium for blocking and access
  • Do not pay ₹4,000 Cr unconditionally for revenue that may disappear because of the acquisition itself
