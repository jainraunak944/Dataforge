The ownership paradox: control impairs the asset and the drag is worth more than the company

Project Satoshi Gate

Paytm × Liminal

1 · Customer conflict

  • Top-4 = ₹132 Cr of ₹220 Cr ARR (CF)
  • Case: ₹66–99 Cr leaves in 12 months = 50–75% of the top-4 book
  • Trigger: a competing consumer wallet owning the custodian

2 · Regulatory capital

  • ₹750 Cr/yr drag = 117% of EBITDA, 156% of PAT, 3.4× Liminal revenue
  • Liminal needs ~₹4,000 Cr ARR (18× today) to self-fund it
  • 16 years away at 20% growth, 11 at 30% (TA)

3 · Earnings

  • Liminal ~breakeven: no earnings to offset the deal’s own cost (CF)
  • Year-1 EPS ₹5.29 (−29%) at the headline mix (TA)
  • The “share-count only” ₹7.38 hides 95% of the dilution

4 · Valuation

  • 18.2× headline → 26–33× retained ARR
  • Each 10 pts of churn destroys ₹400 Cr at the paid multiple
  • Paytm at ~247× trailing P/E: a −29% print invites the buyback debate (CF §3.3)
