A8 · Board decision tree

1 · Is Liminal strategically valuable to Paytm?

YES → continue

NO → walk away

2 · Can Paytm secure access without full control?

YES → 26% + partnership + option

3 · After validation: consents ≥80% and capital treatment compatible with 5-yr FCF breakeven?

YES → test gates 3–6

NO → hold, restructure to JV, or exit

4 · Does control clear valuation, PAT, FCF and compliance gates?

YES → exercise call option

NO → hold minority

End state: control is an earned option, not a day-one commitment.
